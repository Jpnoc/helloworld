Void Shards: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

In short: Void Shards has no accounts and we run no servers. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve those ads, as described below.

Who we are

Void Shards ("the game", "we", "us") is a puzzle game for Android published on Google Play by the developer jpinoc. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • Level progress and stars
  • Void Crystals, boosters, the daily-reward streak and how many reward ads you watched today
  • Sound and music settings

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information. To delete this data, uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Void Shards → Storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch an ad for +5 moves"). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (for example, your country or city)
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version)
  • how you interact with ads, plus diagnostic and performance data

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. Depending on your settings and your region, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is governed by its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the game's title screen.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by device).
  • Watching ads is always optional. Every level can be completed without watching ads.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details. Google Play's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Void Shards is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Data sharing and selling

We do not collect your personal information on our own systems, so we do not sell or share it. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in US states such as California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, the most effective steps are deleting the game's local data (see above), resetting your advertising ID, and using Google's tools at My Ad Center. You can also contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game adds new features. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be noted in the game's Google Play listing.
