HOW TO PLAY

Swap adjacent shards to line up 3 or more of the same kind. Finish every objective before you run out of moves.

4 in a row → Beam Shard (clears a line)
L or T shape → Nova Shard (3×3 blast)
5 in a row → Prism (wipes a whole colour)
Swap two specials together for huge combos.

Threats
⏱ Void Bombs: clear them before the counter hits 0 or the level fails.
☣ Corruption: spreads every turn you don't destroy some. Match next to it.
▣ Obsidian: needs two hits from adjacent matches.
❄ Frost: match on top of it to crack it.
⛓ Chains: locked shards can't move. Match them to break the chain.
